Classify the following as facts or dimensions:

Order amount

Fact or Dimension? - Fact
Reasoning: Why? - Facts capture the measurements that result from business processes
How is it used in analytics? - -- SUM: Total Revenue SELECT SUM(order_amount) AS total_revenue FROM orders;

Quantity sold

Fact or Dimension? - Fact
Reasoning: Why? - Quantity sold is a measurement of business activity
How is it used in analytics? - -- Units sold by productSELECT product_id,SUM(quantity) AS total_units_sold,....

Customer

Fact or Dimension? - Dimension
Reasoning: Why? - Customer is an ENTITY that describes WHO performed the transaction
How is it used in analytics? - -- Customer segments: Group by customer Attributes


Product

Fact or Dimension? - Dimension
Reasoning: Why? - Product is an ENTITY that describes WHAT was purchased
How is it used in analytics? - heirachical Analysis, product Performance, product attributes as filters

Date

Fact or Dimension? - Dimension
Reasoning: Why? - Date is a DIMENSION that describes WHEN the measurement occurred
How is it used in analytics? - time intelligence, calendar vs fiscal, period comparisons

Region

Fact or Dimension? - Dimension
Reasoning: Why? - Region is a DIMENSION that describes WHERE the transaction occurred
How is it used in analytics? - Geographic Analysis, regional Performance, territory Management
